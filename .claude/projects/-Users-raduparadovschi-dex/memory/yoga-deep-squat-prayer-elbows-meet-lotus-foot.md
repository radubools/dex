Hands in prayer at the chest put the elbows at about y=0.46 when the hips are
down at 0.35 — and a half-lotus foot resting on the standing thigh sits at
y=0.45–0.49 too. The right forearm passed 0.8 cm from the left foot: a genuine
intersection that every other check passed straight through.

The fix that worked was the foot, not the arm. A foot laid square across the
thigh points straight out laterally, 0.19 m from an ankle already out at
x=0.066, so the toes reach x=0.25 — right into the hanging forearm. Angling it
along the thigh instead (55% of the thigh axis mixed into the across direction)
swings the toes forward to z=0.31 and opens the gap to 6.9 cm. Resting the foot
a little more distally helps too.

**Why:** the collision is specific to squatting poses — standing with the same
prayer hands has the elbows a long way above any lifted foot — so it will
resurface in every low pose that folds one leg and brings the hands in.

**How to apply:** whenever a pose combines a lifted-and-folded leg with hands at
the chest, measure `skeleton.bone_gap` for the foot bone against both arm bones
before believing the figure. Prefer reorienting the foot to flaring the elbows;
anjali mudra with the elbows drawn out looks wrong. See
[[check-rendered-frames-for-overlap]] for the same lesson in another medium:
passing timing or landmark checks says nothing about things occupying the same
space.
